How It Works

Three Steps to Complete Trust

From kitchen to customer — a seamless hygiene verification loop that builds confidence at every table.

01

Restaurants Get Verified

Vendors complete hygiene checklists, upload proof photos, and earn their KleanMark trust score — updated in real time.

02

Customers Scan

Diners scan the KleanMark QR at the table to instantly see the hygiene score, proof photos, and checklist in full detail.

03

Earn Rewards

Customers check in via OTP to earn loyalty stars — redeem for free food, drinks, and exclusive perks at every visit.
